02 / THE NATURAL WORLD

A different
side of me.

I love wildflowers, moths, butterflies, and the tiny intricacies of the natural world. There is an entirely different kind of beauty in slowing down enough to notice things.

I imagine quiet places filled with flowers and warm afternoon sun, where every little flicker in the air seems alive. Nature can be both a refuge and a fascinating system to study.

“Give me warm golden light, an open field, and the smallest details nature has to offer.”
