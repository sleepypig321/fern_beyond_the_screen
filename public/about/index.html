THE PERSON BEYOND THE PORTRAIT

More than
a portrait.

I don't think a person can be reduced to a few flattering adjectives or a perfectly composed photograph.

I'm very analytical, logical, and independent. I usually try to understand the reasoning behind things before drawing a conclusion, and I value the freedom to make my own choices. I'm curious, always learning something, and drawn to conversations that make me think. I like thinking about things deeply, exploring new ideas, and finding surprising connections between subjects that don't seem related.

I'm a pretty serious person, and I tend to take things at face value. I don't always pick up on the meaning behind a joke, and teasing doesn't always land well with me. But that doesn't mean I don't have a sense of humor—mine can be crude, dry, and a little unexpected. I appreciate people who can communicate directly and understand that distinction.

The parts of me worth getting to know are better understood through conversations, shared curiosity, and how we treat each other than through a checklist.

IN MY OWN WORDS

Independent doesn't mean uninterested. Serious doesn't mean humorless. Getting to know me means making room for a mind that works a little differently.

So here's an invitation to explore a little more, at your own pace.
