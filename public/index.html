01 / The person behind the profile

Not easily
summarized.

Some people can fit themselves into a neat little box. I've never been particularly good at that.

I'm Fern. I'm independent, curious, creative, and usually in the middle of learning something new. I can be completely absorbed in an ambitious idea one minute and laughing at something ridiculous the next.

I'm fascinated by how things work: the universe, intelligence, technology, living systems, and the tiny details most people overlook. I love having room to explore an idea without knowing exactly where it will lead.

Being my own person matters to me. I value the kind of connection where both people can be fully themselves—without a performance, a script, or the expectation that independence and affection must be opposites.

03 / Beyond attraction

Connection
without a script.

For me, genuine chemistry starts with being able to show up as yourself.

Intellectual chemistry.

Someone who enjoys a real conversation, can be curious with me, and isn't afraid to consider a different perspective.

Individuality.

I appreciate people with a life of their own, who respect that independence is a strength rather than an absence of care.

Humor & ease.

Being able to laugh together, be a little strange, and enjoy the unplanned moments matters more than sounding impressive.

Mutual respect.

Clear communication, thoughtful boundaries, and genuine interest in understanding one another.

04 / Beyond the small talk

Ask me
something good.

I'd rather start with an interesting question than pretend a standard introduction tells us much about one another.

Where would you disappear for a day?

Somewhere with wildflowers, butterflies and moths, and late-afternoon sunshine turning everything amber. I think quiet places can be extraordinary.

What's a question you keep returning to?

What intelligence and consciousness actually are—and how much we still have to discover about both.

What could you talk about for hours?

An idea worth exploring. It could start with a scientific question, a design concept, or a completely unexpected observation.

What's something people don't always expect?

That an independent, serious-minded person can also be playful, affectionate in her own way, and very capable of laughing at herself.
