Conversation

A few honest questions and conversation starters.

BEYOND SMALL TALK

A better
first question.

These are some things I'd actually enjoy talking about. Open whichever catches your eye.

Where would you disappear for a day?

Somewhere full of flowers, butterflies and moths, with sunlight turning everything gold. I think quiet places can be extraordinary.

What's a question you keep returning to?

What consciousness actually is, and how much about intelligence we still don't understand.

What could you talk about for hours?

Almost any idea worth exploring. A scientific mystery, a new invention, or a completely unexpected observation can be enough.

What's something people don't always expect?

That I can be intensely independent and serious-minded while also being playful, funny, and happy to laugh at myself.

What's a great way to start talking with you?

Tell me something you're genuinely curious about—or something that makes you uniquely you.
