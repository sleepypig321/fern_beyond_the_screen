03 / WHAT I VALUE

Connection

The qualities and values I appreciate when getting to know someone.

♡
BEYOND ATTRACTION

What feels
meaningful.

I'm not looking for a perfect script. I'm interested in the quality of a connection, and in how two people make room for each other's individuality.

INTELLECTUAL CHEMISTRY

Curiosity is attractive.

I enjoy people who ask good questions, listen well, and can have a thoughtful conversation even when we don't agree.

INDIVIDUALITY

Two complete people.

I value my own independence and appreciate someone who has interests, friendships, and a life of their own.

EASE & HUMOR

We should be able to laugh.

I like people who don't take themselves too seriously, even when they care deeply about something.

RESPECT

Boundaries matter.

Clear intentions, genuine consideration, and respecting each other's pace make connection feel possible.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Something
in common?

If we value some of the same things, I'd rather hear a genuine hello than a polished opening line.
