04 / EVERYDAY LIFE

A chapter
of me.

Away from projects and big questions, I appreciate sweet coffee, time with my cats, my own space, and people who make ordinary moments enjoyable. I can be deeply focused one minute and cracking up over something ridiculous the next.

Independence matters to me. I appreciate genuine connection without pressure, clear communication, and mutual respect for the lives we each have beyond a relationship. I'm a very analytical and logical person, and I can be more serious than people expect. Sometimes I don't quite understand why a joke is supposed to be funny, and I don't always respond well to being teased. My own humor, though? It can be pretty crude and delightfully unexpected.

“I'm serious-minded, independent, and occasionally the person making the most inappropriate joke in the room.”
