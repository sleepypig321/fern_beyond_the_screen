01 / THE COSMOS

A chapter
of me.

I'm endlessly interested in the things we still can't fully explain. Astrophysics, intelligence, consciousness, and the scale of the universe draw me in because they remind me how much remains to discover.

I like having my assumptions challenged thoughtfully. An evening spent talking about an improbable possibility can be more interesting to me than a perfectly planned night out.

“Some of my favorite conversations begin with a question neither person knows how to answer.”
